How it works

Sensors, software and light—built for real galleries.

01 · Sense

It sees you point and wave, not you

Sensors track your hand gestures in front of the wall while protecting your privacy. No images, faces or personal data are ever stored.

02 · Process

SurfaceWare™ reads your intent

Your pointing behaves like a "click" and a wave behaves like a "scroll," turning natural gestures into effortless navigation.

03 · Project

The projector renders the content

The projector renders and updates the content beside the work as you explore more about the artifact, artist and history.

  • No UV or IR output. Filtered optics add no ultraviolet light or radiant heat beside the work.
  • Dims when nobody's there. Occupancy sensing lowers the light whenever the gallery is empty.
  • Nothing to touch or clean. No shared surfaces, no smudges, no reprinted labels.
  • Private by design. Gestures are processed on site and no video is kept.
