How it works

Sensors, software and light, all kept away from the art.

01 · Sense

It sees you arrive

A presence sensor wakes the label. A depth camera reads where you point and how you scroll in front of the wall.

02 · Process

SurfaceWare™ reads intent

Hover OS turns your gestures into commands on site, and SurfaceWare Central manages every device from one portal. No video frames are stored.

03 · Project

A laser draws the label

A ceiling-mounted ultra-short-throw laser renders the label beside the work and dims when the room is empty.

  • No UV or IR output. Filtered optics add no ultraviolet light or radiant heat beside the work.
  • Dims when nobody's there. Occupancy sensing lowers the light whenever the gallery is empty.
  • Nothing to touch or clean. No shared surfaces, no smudges, no reprinted labels.
  • Private by design. Gestures are processed on site and no video is kept.
